Safety

Flicker warning

The user's screen flickers at 11 to 17 Hz, mostly in the range most likely to trigger photosensitive seizures, with up to four squares at once. Nobody with photosensitive epilepsy or flicker sensitivity should look at it.

Esc on the flicker display or Stop here turns the flicker off immediately.

While the session runs, the microphone listens to the conversation partner and Chrome sends the speech to Google to turn it into text. Press Mute any time.
