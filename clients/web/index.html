Photosensitivity warning

      
This page shows squares that flicker at 12 to 20 times a second. Flicker between 15 and
        25 Hz is the range most likely to trigger a seizure in people with photosensitive
        epilepsy.

      
Do not continue if you or anyone who can see this screen has epilepsy or has had a
        seizure. Stop at once if you feel unwell, dizzy or see strange visual effects.

      
Press Esc or the Pause button at any time to stop the
        flicker. It also stops when you lock the screen or switch away from this page, and it
        does not start again until you press Resume.
